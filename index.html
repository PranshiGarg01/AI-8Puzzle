THE MISSION

WHAT IS THE 8-PUZZLE?

The 8-Puzzle is a classic AI problem played on a 3 × 3 grid. Eight numbered tiles and one empty space can slide into neighbouring positions. The goal is to reach a target arrangement using a sequence of valid moves.

▤

STATE SPACE

Each board arrangement is a state. A legal move creates a successor state, forming a graph of possible configurations.

⌘

SEARCH STRATEGY

Search algorithms explore states in different orders to find a path from the initial board to the goal board.
